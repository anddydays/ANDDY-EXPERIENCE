Gesture Manual

These are the gestures recognized by the original detector.

🤙 RockstarThumb + pinky extended while the middle fingers stay curled.
☝️ One finger upOnly the index finger extended and held away from the mouth.
✊ FistClose one hand into a clear fist.
🤫 ShhhPoint one index finger and bring the fingertip close to your mouth.
👉👈 Fingers togetherPoint both index fingers and bring the fingertips close together.
🙈 Cover facePlace one open hand over the central face / mouth area.
😾 Crash outRaise both hands close to the sides of your face.
🙆 Hands on headRaise both hands above and onto the top of your head.
🫴 Open palmHold one open palm outward and away from your face.
👀 Side eyeTurn your head clearly to either side without a stronger hand gesture.
Hold the pose briefly. The original detector waits for 5 stable frames before changing the cat.
